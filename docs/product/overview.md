# Product overview

[Docs home](../README.md) · [User flows](user-flows.md) · [System architecture](../engineering/system-architecture.md)

Alcove provides end-to-end encrypted live video calls with built-in transcripts and notes. No bot joins
the meeting. By default, NEAR AI’s confidential models transcribe and write notes after Alcove checks
their hardware evidence. The public beta runs on Cloud Run. The API can decrypt stored content to
provide the service and does not run in an attested confidential environment.

## The problem

Meeting notes today come from one of three places, and each is wrong for sensitive conversations:

1. **The platform's own AI** (Google Meet, Zoom). Convenient, but the provider reads everything.
2. **A note-taker bot** someone brings in (Fireflies and the like). It sits visibly in the call (imagine a
   therapy session), and sharing its notes with non-users pushes them to sign up.
3. **Recording in the background yourself.** Illegal under two-party consent in many places, uncomfortable
   for everyone else, and fiddly to set up.

Proton Meet shows that end-to-end encrypted calls can be pleasant and open source, but it has no AI notes.
Alcove's thesis: confidential transcription and notes belong inside a privacy-first video platform.

## What it does

| Capability | What the user gets |
| --- | --- |
| Sign-in | Sign in with Google. Connect Google Calendar separately. Microsoft and SSO are demo flows for local development and Storybook. |
| Calls | Start a call now or schedule one from a single line; join full screen with camera, mic (each with its own device menu, a level meter, and plain help when the browser blocks them), each person's volume for you, video quality (send up to HD 1080p; see cameras as sharp as their tiles, at their smallest, or not at all), screen sharing (a tab, window or screen; several people at once, two side by side; zoom into a share, freeze it, step back through its last pictures, or open it in its own window, for you only), pointing at what's shared (a named pointer and marks that fade, end-to-end encrypted), a board everyone in the call works on at once (start one from a template or bring one your team has, open just for you until you present it, asked first about anyone who can't open it; beside a shared screen; a board in each breakout room, sealed to the room, coming back to the main board as rooms close, or one board every room shares), presenting your screen, a board or the workspace (everyone follows, anyone can unfollow to work alongside, and closing is for you or, for whoever presents, for everyone), raise hand, captions in your own language, chat, your own view (no layouts to choose: the stage lays itself out, and you pin several people, drag tiles into another order, make one bigger from its corner, and see yourself in the grid, a corner or not at all; beside what's open the people stand at the window's right edge in a column, or a row of arches on a narrow stage, notes, chat and people open beside what's open rather than over them, and the seam beside the people folds them into a rail of arches, remembered in this browser; notes, chat or people beside the call, one at a time; all by keyboard too), spotlight and breakout rooms for the organizer, who can also close anyone's presentation or stop anyone's share for everyone. Move a call to another device (a QR code or link; your place, microphone and camera come with you), and come back by yourself after a dropped connection. Scheduled calls go on the organizer's calendar, which invites everyone, and an organizer can move one of their own calendar events to Alcove. |
| Notes in calls | On or off for each call, set when it's scheduled (new calls follow the account's default). During the call only the organizer can switch them or pause them, and anyone else can ask for them. Everyone in the call, and anyone about to join, sees what's captured, in a sentence that says what isn't working: recording and transcript (both of everyone the host hears), AI notes and shared screens are each on, paused, off, or unavailable when the host's browser can't capture it or is out of the call. The host is the organizer, or while the organizer's connection is down or they've left it to someone, someone invited standing in; the call goes on, and the organizer takes it back by joining again. While they're on, what's said is transcribed as the call goes. Nothing is recorded while notes are off or paused, and the notes say where they were, and where the organizer's browser was out of the call. The organizer can also have shared screens read: each presenter's own browser sends what changes on their share, and the notes show what was on screen beside what was said, and point at it. |
| Catching up in a call | For you alone: what you missed while away or before you joined, or any stretch of the call, sorted into decided, suggested, still open and next, each point with the moments it was said, saying what it couldn't hear, what it skipped in a very long stretch, and as of when. Nothing is kept unless you keep an answer in your own notes. In a breakout room it reads what was said in the room while you were there. Everyone on the invite (people signed in whom the organizer let in from the link included) catches up on the whole call; guests with only the link and a name catch up on what was said while they were there. |
| Who gets the notes | Everyone invited, when the call ends, and people signed in whom the organizer lets in from the link (they're added to the invite). They find the notes in Alcove when they sign in with that address, and anyone who has them can share them on by email or with one of their groups, or by a link that gives them to whoever opens it signed in and says so, for seven days. Each note records how everyone got it: invited by the organizer, or shared afterwards by whom, with which group or by whose link. Guests with only the link and a name don't get them. |
| Recording in the room | Record an in-person conversation into the same kind of notes, with or without a connection: offline, it's kept on the device, encrypted, and uploads when the connection is back. They stay yours alone until you share them. |
| Workspace and the Drive | A call uses one workspace, which its organizer picks or makes when scheduling it or in the call (nothing makes one by itself), and later calls in its series keep it; a call that had none asks its organizer, once it ends, where its notes go. The call's notes, follow-ups and transcript land there, in a folder of the call's own at the top, live during the call and the written note after it, for whoever has the note. The Drive lists every workspace you're in, newest first, one made for a call with the call and when; any can be shared, and its admins can delete it. Documents and folders a team writes in together, live: a collaborative editor with every kind of block, starting points, a tree you arrange by dragging, Trash for 30 days, sharing a document with people inside or outside the workspace or closing it to them, and roles for who's in it. Writing carries on through a dropped connection: what's written stays in the browser, sealed, and sends on return, and what can't reach a document is never lost: it can be copied, saved as a new document, or let go. Find looks through every document and line you can open, in your browser, so what you type goes nowhere. History goes back through a document call by call, and restoring keeps today's as a version. Ask shortens a selection, turns it into a checklist or says what was said about it, in confidential compute. In a call it opens on your stage, just for you, with the people beside it, and a share can sit beside it: you can see who's where and follow them, tag and comment, and the call's notes, follow-ups and transcript are pages in it. Anyone in it can present it, and everyone follows them to their page and line, free to unfollow and write alongside; the organizer can give the call another workspace, and its notes go there from then on, and someone who can't open it can ask for one document. Afterwards the note says what was worked on, and the next call in a series finds its agenda waiting. Everything is sealed, and nobody gets a name they can't open. |
| Team and groups | The Team page: who at your company uses Alcove (the directory, or the colleagues at your work address you meet with), standing on the horizon, what you have coming up or your last call with each, and Invite for the rest. Groups you make there (or from the side panel) invite a whole group to a call, or share notes with all of them, in one step; only you see them. |
| Notes | Summary, decisions, open questions, follow-ups, chapters and the transcript. Decisions stay proposed until people confirm them, and a follow-up is a suggestion until the person it's for takes it on, each with what was said behind it. Questions are answered from the call only, citing the moments they come from, and questions across all your notes, answered from those notes only, citing the notes and moments. |
| Your own notes | Beside the shared notes, in the call and on the note: what you write for yourself, moments you bookmark, and answers you keep. Only you read them, the organizer included, and every note and AI surface says who it's for before you write. Add something to the shared notes when you choose: you see exactly what goes and who reads it first, and you can take it back. |
| Series | Calls that follow on from each other: scheduled to repeat, with one link, from a recurring calendar event, or by scheduling the next one. Before the next call and during it, what earlier ones decided, left open and handed out shows before joining and beside the call, breakout rooms' included, and on the series page its card opens, each with where it was said, and anyone can ask across the calls, in the call too. When a later call seems to replace a decision or answer a question, the notes say so to whoever has both calls' notes, and people settle it. Everyone sees only the calls whose notes they have: an invite to one call opens nothing from the others. |
| Notifications | While Alcove is open in a tab, the browser tells you a call you organize starts in 1 or 5 minutes, notes are ready, someone shared notes with you, someone asks for notes in your call, or it's a follow-up's due day, at 9:00. Each has its switch in Settings; they show only on that device, from one tab, each once. |
| Privacy | Content is encrypted before it's stored, under keys for each account. The server holds those keys, wrapped under one master key, so the promise that Alcove can't read notes depends on releasing that key only to attested confidential compute. Speech and transcripts go to private inference (NEAR AI Cloud) to be transcribed and written up. Deleting your account deletes everything it owns, the notes you organized for everyone, and takes you off notes other people gave you. |
| Plans | Every account is on Free, with every feature: calls are unlimited, each up to 10 hours and 100 people, with about 5 hours of notes in each 30-day period, 10 GB of storage for uploads and call recordings, files up to 512 MB, and as many shared workspaces as you like. The side panel and Settings › Plan say how much is left: "About 3 h 20 min of notes left", an estimate, since what a call uses varies. When the organizer's notes or storage run out, notes stop for everyone in the call, who see "Notes have stopped"; the organizer alone is warned 5 minutes before, and the call goes on. A balance in dollars can be added to with USDC or USDT. |

## Status of this build

| Area | State |
| --- | --- |
| Sign-in with Google | Working: OpenID Connect with PKCE, accepting only email addresses Google has verified |
| Sign-in with Microsoft and SSO | Demo only, disabled in the public beta. Microsoft signs in a demo person (Art Vandelay). SSO takes example addresses |
| Calendar | When connected, Google Calendar puts meetings with other people in Home's Coming up (unless other meetings are switched off), puts scheduled calls on the calendar with Google's invitations, keeps changes and cancellations in step, and lets organizers make their own events Alcove calls. Demo accounts get a sample day of calls when nothing is upcoming |
| Team | Stand-in directory for `vandelay.industries` accounts. Other work accounts see the colleagues at their email domain they have met on Alcove calls or calendar events; personal domains have no team. Inviting someone opens your own email with the invitation and marks them invited; Alcove sends no email itself |
| Scheduling and instant calls | Working. Alcove sends no emails of its own; invitations come from Google Calendar when it's connected. Calls started now don't go on the calendar |
| Calls with real camera, microphone, screen sharing, chat | Working for groups on LiveKit, end-to-end encrypted (MLS): invited people join with their accounts, anyone else with the link asks and the organizer lets them in. The live transcript and notes hear everyone the organizer hears, each line under who said it |
| Notes recording (encrypted audio pieces, playback) | Working: one recording of everyone the organizer hears, so a line plays back with its speaker's voice |
| Recording without a connection | Working on the stage recorder: kept in the browser, sealed with a key made there, uploaded in order once the connection is back (also after the tab closes), and the app opens offline for someone signed in on that browser. Calls need a connection |
| Live transcript (NEAR AI) | Working while notes are on, in calls and room recordings. Call lines name whoever said them, from everyone the organizer hears; room recordings don't say who spoke. What it isn't sure of (words hard to hear, a name heard on two microphones) is marked, and a written note's transcript can be corrected, by the organizer and by the people who spoke |
| Written notes and questions (NEAR AI) | Working: summary, decisions, open questions, follow-ups, chapters and suggested questions, written when the recording stops; answers cite moments in the recording. Questions across all your notes are answered from up to 12 that share the most words with them, citing each note and moment, and say so without asking a model when no note is written yet. If the notes service fails three times, the note keeps its transcript and audio without a summary. Each model's hardware evidence is checked first and everything sent is sealed to the key it vouches for; the note says which verified models wrote it and what was checked |
| Series | Working on a call's card, the notes, the series page, at pre-join and in the call: calls scheduled to repeat, recurring Google events held in Alcove and calls scheduled as the next one |
| Notes for everyone invited, sharing by email | Working |
| Notes from shared screens | Working: each presenter says whether a share goes in the notes; their own browser records it, sealed as it arrives; after the call it replays with the audio, and frames picked where what was said points at it are read by the verified model the organizer chose, linked to the transcript and referred to by the notes and answers. Catch-up during the call doesn't read screens yet |
| Workspace and the Drive | A home for each call, made for it or chosen when scheduling, with its folder and the people invited; the Drive, newest first. Working in the app and in a call: the list, documents written by several people at once, with photos, films and files in them, starting points, Trash, sharing, people and roles, writing offline, faces, following, tags and comments, Find, History and Ask on a selection; in a call, a call's notes, follow-ups and transcript as pages in its folder, on your side and as blocks in a document, presenting the workspace, playing a film in a document for everyone, the organizer switching what everyone views, asking for a document from outside it, and what was worked on in the note afterwards. Without export |
| Browser notifications | Working while Alcove is open in a tab, shown through the app's service worker. Nothing is pushed when no tab is open |
| Plans and payments | Working: Free for every account ([Plans and usage](../engineering/plans-and-usage.md)). Every NEAR AI request is paid for from the organizer's or the asker's AI allowance, checked and held before it's sent; notes stop for everyone in a call when the organizer's allowance or storage runs out. Storage counts per account, recordings included, with a 15-day grace for an account that's over before uploads stop. Calls end at 10 hours and take 100 people. A balance in dollars is filled with USDC or USDT through NEAR Intents 1Click: choose $20, $60, $120 or your own amount, send exactly that to a one-off address, and it's added at face value when it arrives. Pro and Team aren't built yet, so nothing yet spends the balance |
| Confidential compute (TEE, attestation, master key release) | The Cloud Run beta does not have attested hosting or key release. See the requirements for a [confidential production deployment](../engineering/privacy-keys-and-sessions.md#production-requirements). |
